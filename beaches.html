Golden Beaches

Relax on Ghana's pristine coastal destinations

Labadi Beach, Accra

Location: Greater Accra Region

Labadi Beach is Accra's most popular beach, offering a lively atmosphere with numerous restaurants, bars, and water sports facilities. Perfect for tourists seeking both relaxation and entertainment.

Activities & Facilities:

  • Swimming and sunbathing
  • Water sports (jet skiing, surfing)
  • Beachfront restaurants and cafes
  • Live music and entertainment
  • Beach volleyball and other sports

Best Time to Visit: November to March (dry season)

Busua Beach & Ankobra

Location: Western Region

Busua Beach is known for its excellent surfing conditions and laid-back beach town atmosphere. The area is perfect for backpackers and adventure seekers looking for authentic coastal experiences.

Highlights:

  • World-class surfing waves
  • Relaxed beach town vibe
  • Budget-friendly accommodations
  • Fresh seafood restaurants
  • Sunset viewpoints

Best Time to Visit: October to March (peak surf season)

Cape Coast Beach

Location: Central Region

Cape Coast Beach combines historical tourism with beach relaxation. Visitors can explore the historic castles by day and enjoy fresh seafood and sunset views by evening.

Combined Experiences:

  • Historic castle exploration
  • Beautiful sandy beaches
  • Fresh fish and seafood markets
  • Coastal town atmosphere
  • Scenic sunset viewings

Best Time to Visit: November to March

🏖️ Beach Information

Swimming: Safe and suitable for swimming year-round

Best Times: Early mornings and late afternoons (cooler temperatures)

What to Bring: Sunscreen, hat, swimwear, water shoes

Facilities: Restaurants, restrooms, and changing facilities available at all beaches
